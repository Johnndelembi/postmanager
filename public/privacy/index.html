Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains the information Postmanager uses to provide video planning, optional TikTok publishing, and public post performance. John Ndelembi operates Postmanager and is the contact for privacy questions and requests at williamjohnie61@gmail.com.

TikTok features require your authorization and the permissions approved for the app. Private plans stay on hold until you explicitly approve publishing. AI video generation is not available in this release.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and verification: your phone number, a hashed one-time code, code request and attempt times, a hashed network address for rate limits, and account activity time.
  • Content and plans: uploaded MP4 files, filenames, titles, captions, video length, posting settings, queue times, scheduled times, and draft or planned status.
  • Optional TikTok connection: your TikTok account identifier and display name, granted permissions, and encrypted access and refresh tokens with their expiry times. We do not ask for or store your TikTok password.
  • Publishing and performance: your final publishing approval, its destination account, TikTok publishing and post identifiers, processing status or errors, and views, likes, comments, and shares returned for public posts. We store metric readings with their capture times so you can see changes.
  • Technical information: information needed to serve the site and protect it, such as request metadata and service logs held by our hosting providers.

Metric sync covers posts published through Postmanager. TikTok may not return counts for private, deleted, or otherwise unavailable posts. We keep existing readings with their sync time rather than treating missing data as zero.

2. Why we use it

We use your phone number and verification data to sign you in, your files and post details to provide the planner you request, and technical and rate-limit data to keep sign-in and storage working safely. If you connect TikTok and grant the required permissions, we use the account information and tokens to request creator settings, submit content you approve, check publishing status, and periodically retrieve public post metrics. Publishing approval is tied to the TikTok account you reviewed. These uses support our service agreement with you and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse. Where a legal obligation applies, we may process information to meet it.

We do not currently use your drafts or videos to train an AI model, sell your personal information, or use advertising trackers in the app.

3. Providers and overseas processing

We use Beem to send verification SMS messages, so Beem receives your phone number and the message. Neon stores account and planner records, encrypted TikTok tokens, metric history, and private video objects. Netlify hosts the website, API, publishing tasks, and temporary video delivery. If you connect TikTok, TikTok handles authorization and supplies the account information, posting settings, status, and metrics you permit. When you explicitly approve publishing, TikTok receives the video, caption, and posting settings. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law.

Our Neon database and video storage are configured in a United States region. Your information may therefore be stored and processed outside Tanzania. Netlify and other providers may also process request data in locations where they operate. Contact us if you want more information about a particular transfer.

4. Cookies and security

Postmanager uses an essential, signed, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in. It expires after up to 24 hours of inactivity. When you connect TikTok, a separate secure, HTTP-only cookie protects that authorization step and expires after up to 10 minutes. The current app does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Videos are kept in a private Neon bucket. Upload and preview links are signed and short-lived, and the app checks account ownership before issuing a preview. For an approved publishing attempt, we create a signed delivery link valid for up to two hours so TikTok can download that video. Anyone holding that temporary link can access the video during its validity; the bucket remains private. We use access controls and HTTPS in the deployed app, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. How long we keep information

Verification codes expire after 60 seconds. Verification and network-limit records may remain after code expiry to help prevent abuse and are removed during operational cleanup. Account details, optional TikTok connection records, publishing approvals and status, metric history, drafts, plans, and uploaded files remain while needed to provide the service or handle a valid request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Deleting a draft in the planner removes the post record, but its uploaded video may remain in private storage. To request removal of your account or associated files, email us. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

6. Your choices and rights

You can edit drafts and move planned posts back to drafts to withdraw publishing approval before processing starts. Content already sent to TikTok is subject to TikTok's own controls and policies. You may revoke Postmanager's access in TikTok and email us to request removal of the stored account connection or metric history. To request access to, correction of, export of, restriction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to a use of it, contact williamjohnie61@gmail.com. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. We will respond in line with applicable law after verifying your request.

You may also complain to Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission. We use automated limits to protect sign-in, but do not currently make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects solely by automation.

7. Children

Postmanager is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly offer accounts to children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. The date above shows the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, contact John Ndelembi at williamjohnie61@gmail.com.
