Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy explains the information Postmanager uses to provide a private video-planning workspace. John Ndelembi operates Postmanager and is the contact for privacy questions and requests at williamjohnie61@gmail.com.

This policy describes the current release. TikTok account connection, publishing, analytics sync, and AI video generation are not active in the Netlify app yet. We will update this policy before those features process additional data.

1. Information we collect

  • Account and verification: your phone number, a hashed one-time code, code request and attempt times, a hashed network address for rate limits, and account activity time.
  • Content and plans: uploaded MP4 files, filenames, titles, captions, video length, posting settings, queue times, scheduled times, and draft or planned status.
  • Technical information: information needed to serve the site and protect it, such as request metadata and service logs held by our hosting providers.

We do not currently collect TikTok account credentials or post analytics through the Netlify app.

2. Why we use it

We use your phone number and verification data to sign you in, your files and post details to provide the planner you request, and technical and rate-limit data to keep sign-in and storage working safely. These uses support our service agreement with you and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse. Where a legal obligation applies, we may process information to meet it. Future optional integrations will have their own permission steps.

We do not currently use your drafts or videos to train an AI model, sell your personal information, or use advertising trackers in the app.

3. Providers and overseas processing

We use Beem to send verification SMS messages, so Beem receives your phone number and the message. Neon stores account and planner records and private video objects. Netlify hosts the website and API. These providers process information needed to deliver their services. We may also disclose information if required by applicable law.

Our Neon database and video storage are configured in a United States region. Your information may therefore be stored and processed outside Tanzania. Netlify and other providers may also process request data in locations where they operate. Contact us if you want more information about a particular transfer.

4. Cookies and security

Postmanager uses an essential, signed, HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in. It expires after up to 24 hours of inactivity. The current app does not set advertising or analytics cookies.

Videos are kept in a private Neon bucket. Upload and preview links are signed and short-lived, and the app checks account ownership before issuing a preview. We use access controls and HTTPS in the deployed app, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

5. How long we keep information

Verification codes expire after 60 seconds. Verification and network-limit records may remain after code expiry to help prevent abuse and are removed during operational cleanup. Account details, drafts, plans, and uploaded files remain while needed to provide the service or handle a valid request, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Deleting a draft in the planner removes the post record, but its uploaded video may remain in private storage. To request removal of your account or associated files, email us. We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder.

6. Your choices and rights

You can edit drafts and move planned posts back to drafts in the app. To request access to, correction of, export of, restriction of, or deletion of your personal information, or to object to a use of it, contact williamjohnie61@gmail.com. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing. We will respond in line with applicable law after verifying your request.

You may also complain to Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission. We use automated limits to protect sign-in, but do not currently make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects solely by automation.

7. Children

Postmanager is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly offer accounts to children. If you believe a child has provided information, please contact us so we can review and remove it where appropriate.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the product changes. The date above shows the latest version. For privacy questions or requests, contact John Ndelembi at williamjohnie61@gmail.com.
