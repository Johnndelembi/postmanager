A place to plan the next TikTok video.

Postmanager gives creators a private workspace for video drafts and a clear posting plan. Prepare a caption, keep the MP4 with its draft, and choose a queue slot or exact date in East Africa Time.

Open your workspaceHow we handle your data

What you can do today

  • Sign in with a one-time SMS code.
  • Upload an MP4 to private storage and create or edit a draft.
  • Set preferred queue times, add a draft to the next slot, or schedule a specific time.
  • Move a planned post back to drafts whenever you need to revise it.

What is being prepared

TikTok account connection, publishing, post analytics, and guided video generation are still in development. A queued or scheduled item is a private plan and will not automatically publish to TikTok. You will review and approve publishing separately when that feature is available.
