A place to plan and publish the next TikTok video.

Postmanager gives creators a private workspace for video drafts and a clear posting plan. Prepare a caption, keep the MP4 with its draft, and choose a queue slot or exact date in East Africa Time. With the required TikTok permissions, approve a post for publishing and follow its public performance.

What you can do today

  • Sign in with a one-time SMS code.
  • Upload an MP4 to private storage and create or edit a draft.
  • Set preferred queue times, add a draft to the next slot, or schedule a specific time.
  • Move a planned post back to drafts to cancel approval before publishing starts.
  • Optionally connect a TikTok account and, when permissions and video delivery are ready, review and approve publishing now or when a post is due.
  • Sync views, likes, comments, and shares returned by TikTok for public posts, with a history of recorded readings.

Publishing and performance

A private plan stays on hold until you explicitly approve the final video, caption, settings, and destination TikTok account. Approved posts can publish automatically when due. TikTok app approval, account permissions, and verified video delivery are required. Account changes or unavailable settings can hold a post for another review, and posting times are subject to processing and service availability.

Performance includes the four counts TikTok makes available for public posts sent through Postmanager. Private or unavailable posts may have no metrics, and existing readings may be out of date. Guided AI video generation is not available yet.
